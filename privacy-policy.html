Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Mowing Pros USA ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through mowingprosusa.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you request a quote or contact us, we collect the information you voluntarily provide: your name, email address, phone number, property address, the service(s) you're interested in, and how you heard about us. Once you become a client, we also collect service-related details necessary to perform and manage your service, such as property notes, service history, and scheduling information, through our field service management platform (Jobber).

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote request and schedule service
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your request or appointment
  • To send you service reminders, invoices, or — only if you've opted in — promotional offers
  • To improve our services and website
  • To analyze website traffic and usage, as described under "Cookies & Tracking" below

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. No mobile information — including opt-in data and consent — will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We use a small number of third-party services to run this website and respond to you:

  • Formspree — receives and stores the information submitted through our quote form
  • EmailJS — sends the automatic confirmation email you receive after submitting a quote request
  • Jobber — our field service management and client relationship platform, used to schedule appointments, manage client and service records, send service-related texts and emails, and process invoices and payments through its integrated payment processor
  • Quo — our business phone and text messaging platform, used to send and receive calls and texts related to your quote, appointments, and service updates
  • Subcontracted crews — from time to time, services at your property may be performed by a vetted subcontractor working on our behalf rather than a direct Mowing Pros USA employee. Subcontractors receive only the information necessary to complete the scheduled work (such as your service address and service instructions) and are required to keep your information confidential.

We do not share your information with any other third party except as necessary to provide our services or as required by law.

Payment Information

If you set up billing for recurring or one-time service, your payment details (such as credit card information) are collected and processed directly by Jobber and its integrated payment processor. We do not store your full payment card information on our own systems.

Text Messages & Calls

By submitting your phone number, you agree we may contact you about your specific quote or appointment. If you separately check the consent box on our quote form, you also agree to receive calls and texts from Mowing Pros USA about your quote and appointments, including automated messages, at the number provided. Message and data rates may apply and frequency of messages may vary. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out of texts at any time, and you can ask us to stop calling at any time. Consent to receive texts or calls is never a condition of purchasing our services.

We comply with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and applicable telemarketing and messaging laws in how we collect and use your consent to contact you. We maintain timestamped opt-in records for all communication consent you provide through our website.

Cookies & Tracking

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website — for example, which pages are viewed and how visitors found our site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information in aggregate, and we do not use it to identify you individually. Like most websites, we also automatically receive certain technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser type, and device information — typically collected through Google Analytics as described above. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings. We do not currently use advertising or retargeting cookies.

Your Rights

You may ask us at any time to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or to request that we restrict certain uses of your information, by contacting us using the information below.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative precautions to protect the information you share with us, including relying on established, reputable third-party services (Formspree, EmailJS, Jobber, and Quo) that maintain their own security standards. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain the information you submit for as long as necessary to respond to your request, provide service, and meet our recordkeeping and legal obligations. You may request deletion of your information at any time as described under "Your Rights" above.

Business Transfers

If Mowing Pros USA is ever involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. It would remain subject to the protections described in this policy.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies separately. This policy applies only to information collected by Mowing Pros USA.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults arranging lawn care for their property. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent changes. Continued use of this site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
